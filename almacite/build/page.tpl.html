Cite the ALMA receivers

Tick the bands you observed in. Out comes an acknowledgement sentence and the BibTeX, ready to paste into your manuscript.

References follow ALMA Memo 627 (Bakx & Conway 2024), which compiled them in direct contact with the receiver groups. ALMA's receivers underpin nearly ten thousand papers; the receiver papers themselves are cited a few dozen times at best. That gap is an information gap, and this page exists to close it in one click.
