How to play

Each piece is a real ALMA image of a gravitationally lensed galaxy, blown up 1300× so you can see it — and it starts badly stretched, so slide it brighter until the ring or arc appears.

Every one of them sits on a source in the Herschel/SPIRE maps below — but Herschel's beam is far too coarse to tell them apart, so the board shows each unfound galaxy as a faint preview at its true spot. Match the shape, drag the piece there, and it clicks into place.

Coordinates draws the RA/Dec grid and puts each piece's sky position in its panel — the astronomer's way to find it. Positions just rings the spots that are still empty. Scroll or pinch to zoom; drag empty sky to pan.
